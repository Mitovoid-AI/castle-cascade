Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information Castle Cascade (“the game”), made by Mitovoid AI (“we”), uses and why. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device.
  • The iOS and Android apps show ads from Google AdMob, which uses device information to show and measure ads (details below). The web version shows no ads.
  • In the apps you can buy Castle Cascade Pro, once or as a subscription. Payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store and recorded with RevenueCat; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your information.

What the game stores

  • Game progress: your level, coins, stars, boosters, lives, castle rooms, achievements and statistics.
  • Settings: sound and vibration choices.
  • Profile: the display name and avatar you pick. The game has no multiplayer, so no other player sees them.

This is kept in your device’s storage for the game. It stays there until you reset your progress or remove the game.

Cloud save (web version): if you play on a website while signed in to an account with that service, your progress is also saved to that account under an anonymous ID, so it follows you to other devices. It holds only the game progress listed above.

Advertising

The iOS and Android apps use Google AdMob to show ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional ads between levels. To serve, limit and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad the app asks your permission before using your device’s advertising identifier; if you say no, you still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Google’s use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Purchases in the apps are processed by Google Play (Android) or Apple’s App Store (iOS). We use RevenueCat to confirm and record purchases so that Castle Cascade Pro can be restored on a new device and subscriptions can be managed in the app. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history, your device type and the highest level you have reached in the game, which helps us understand which players buy. We never receive your card or bank details. The store’s privacy policy applies to the payment, and RevenueCat’s is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or files, and it does not track you across other apps or websites beyond what the ad provider described above does.

How the information is used

  • To run the game and remember your progress.
  • To show ads and keep their frequency reasonable.
  • To deliver and restore what you buy, and to understand which players buy.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. It is shared only with the services that make the game work (Google AdMob for ads; Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat for purchases), or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Open Settings → Reset progress to erase your saved game, including the cloud copy. Removing the game or clearing its storage also deletes it. If you want us to delete anything else, email mitovoid.contact@gmail.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact mitovoid.contact@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the little information the game handles, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Because your progress lives on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time. For anything else, email mitovoid.contact@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
